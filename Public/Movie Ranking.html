My Top 5 Favorite Movies

The Shawshank Redemption

The Shawshank Redemption is a 1994 American drama film written and directed by Frank Darabont, based on the 1982 novella Rita Hayworth and Shawshank Redemption by Stephen King. It stars Tim Robbins as Andy Dufresne and Morgan Freeman as Ellis "Red" Redemption, two imprisoned men who bond over a number of years, finding solace and eventual redemption through acts of common decency.

The Godfather

The Godfather is a 1972 American crime drama film directed by Francis Ford Coppola, based on the 1969 novel of the same name by Mario Puzo. It stars Marlon Brando as Don Vito Corleone and Al Pacino as his son, Michael Corleone, who rise to power within the fictional Italian-American Mafia family.

The Dark Knight

The Dark Knight is a 2008 superhero film directed, produced, and co-written by Christopher Nolan. Based on the DC Comics character Batman, the film is the second installment of Nolan's The Dark Knight Trilogy and a sequel to 2005's Batman Begins. It stars Christian Bale as Bruce Wayne / Batman, Heath Ledger as the Joker, Aaron Eckhart as Harvey Dent, Michael Caine as Alfred Pennyworth, Maggie Gyllenhaal as Rachel Dawes, and Gary Oldman as James Gordon.

Pulp Fiction

Pulp Fiction is a 1994 American crime film written and directed by Quentin Tarantino, who conceived it with Roger Avary. Starring John Travolta, Samuel L. Jackson, Bruce Willis, Tim Roth, Ving Rhames, and Uma Thurman, it tells several stories of criminal Los Angeles. The title refers to the pulp magazines and hardboiled crime novels popular in the mid-20th century, known for their graphic violence and punchy dialogue.
